# 📊 Walmart Sales Dashboard & 10-Day Sales Forecast

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built using **Power BI** to analyze Walmart sales performance, profitability, customer segments, shipping methods, payment preferences, and future sales trends through forecasting.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Walmart's sales data and helps stakeholders make data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations and forecasting.

The project focuses on:

- Sales Performance Analysis
- Profitability Analysis
- Customer Segment Insights
- Geographic Sales Distribution
- Shipping & Payment Analysis
- Time-Series Trend Analysis
- 10-Day Sales Forecasting

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

## 📊 Key Insights

### Sales Performance
- Total Sales exceeded **$1.57 Million**
- Total Profit reached **$175K+**
- More than **22K products sold**

### Customer Insights
- Consumer Segment generated the highest sales.
- Corporate Segment contributed significantly to revenue.

### Category Analysis
- Office Supplies led overall sales.
- Technology and Furniture remained strong contributors.

### Payment Analysis
- Cash on Delivery (COD) was the most preferred payment method.
- Online payments showed strong adoption.

### Shipping Analysis
- Standard Class dominated shipment volume.
- Same-Day delivery had the lowest share.

### Geographic Insights
- California generated the highest sales.
- New York and Texas followed among top-performing states.

---

## 🔮 Forecasting

Power BI's forecasting capability was used to predict sales for the next 10 days based on historical trends.

Benefits:
- Future demand estimation
- Better inventory planning
- Improved business decision-making
- Trend identification
